Metacognition

Metacognition is "thinking about thinking" being aware of how you learn, plan, and check your own understanding instead of just absorbing information passively. It covers things like noticing when you've actually understood something versus just recognized it, planning your approach before starting a task, and reflecting on what worked afterward.
