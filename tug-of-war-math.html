How to Play Tug of War: Math

Tug of War: Math is a free two-team multiplication and times-tables game you can play right in your browser — no download needed. Split your class or family into Team Blue and Team Crimson, or practice against the computer! Each team gets its own question and number pad. Every correct answer pulls the rope toward that team's side; the first team to reach the target score wins the pull.

Why it's great for practicing math facts

Fast, low-pressure repetition is one of the best ways to build fluency with multiplication tables and mental math. The head-to-head format keeps students engaged, works on any phone, tablet, or classroom display, and lets you adjust the number range and win score to match the level of your students.
